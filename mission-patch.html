Concept & symbols

Through collaboration and trial and error, curiosity becomes a path to discovery. The puzzle pieces represent people bringing their ideas together, while the cycle reflects the process of testing, learning, and trying again. Together, these symbols show how shared effort and a willingness to experiment can turn questions into new possibilities.

The eye and magnifying glass suggest observation and discovery. The road represents curiosity and the journey forward. Bubbles suggest weightlessness, while the flag marks a new discovery. Geometric forms translate these ideas into simpler visual relationships.

How variations work

Every patch keeps collaboration, experimentation, and discovery together. One eye or magnifier leads the design; puzzle pieces and cycle arrows support it. A road, bubbles, flag, or geometric forms can add one supporting idea.

Generate changes the visual language, arrangement, and discovery symbol within your choices. Remix keeps those decisions and adjusts spacing, proportions, and puzzle rotations.

Use Colors & lettering to customize the patch palette, text, and borders. Individual elements can be resized, moved, rotated, recolored, or hidden. Generate and Remix keep your custom element adjustments. The small preview mirrors the exported artwork. PNG exports are 1050 × 1050 pixels at 300 dpi; SVG exports are 3.5 × 3.5 inches.
