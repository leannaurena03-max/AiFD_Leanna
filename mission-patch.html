Concept & symbols

Through collaboration and trial and error, curiosity becomes a path to discovery. The puzzle pieces represent people bringing their ideas together, while the cycle reflects the process of testing, learning, and trying again. Together, these symbols show how shared effort and a willingness to experiment can turn questions into new possibilities.

The eye and magnifying glass suggest observation and discovery. The road represents curiosity and the journey forward. Floating spheres suggest weightlessness, while the flag marks a new discovery. Geometric forms translate these ideas into simpler visual relationships.

How variations work

The generator combines symbols from both sketch sheets. It changes the main symbol, supporting symbols, composition, proportions, rotations, and visual language.

Choose a specific language or composition to explore it further, or leave both on Explore for a wider range. Your labels, palette, and enabled elements stay as you set them.
