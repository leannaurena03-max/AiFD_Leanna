FAHERTY — BRAND SKILLS
Stay a Little Longer
Fall / Winter Campaign · New York City
01. Campaign Identity
Brand: Faherty Campaign: Stay a Little Longer Season: Late Fall / Early Winter Location: New York City waterfront Category: Smart-casual winter fleece and everyday layering
Campaign Concept
"Stay a Little Longer" is a seasonal lifestyle campaign centered around the transition from autumn into winter in New York City.
The campaign celebrates the small moments that make the colder months enjoyable: walking along the waterfront, meeting friends for coffee, watching the skyline at sunset, and spending a little more time outside even when the temperature drops.
Fleece is the central product, presented as an essential part of an everyday winter wardrobe.
The campaign should communicate comfort, warmth, quality, and effortless style without looking overly luxurious or formal.
Core feeling: Comfortable enough to stay, stylish enough to go anywhere.
02. Brand Personality
The visual identity should feel:
	•	Warm, approachable, and welcoming
	•	Relaxed but thoughtfully styled
	•	Smart casual rather than formal or athletic
	•	Classic and timeless with subtle contemporary details
	•	Naturally fashionable without appearing overly curated
	•	Nostalgic, peaceful, and slightly cinematic
	•	Understated, with occasional statement pieces
The campaign should feel like real people enjoying New York City rather than models posing for a traditional fashion advertisement.

Color rules:
	•	Approximately 75–85% of the visual styling should use neutral and earthy colors.
	•	The remaining 15–25% may introduce muted seasonal accents.
	•	Burgundy and red should complement the neutrals rather than overpower them.

	•	Preserve natural skin tones and accurate product colors.
	•	The clothing should stand out through texture, layering, and silhouette rather than excessive color.
04. Fashion & Styling
Overall Wardrobe
Faherty's winter fleece should be styled as an everyday wardrobe essential that works for casual outings, weekend activities, and relaxed city environments.
The styling should be smart casual, balancing comfort and sophistication without appearing corporate or overly dressed up.
Essential Pieces
	•	Olive, brown, cream, and oatmeal fleece jackets
	•	Quarter-zip fleece pullovers
	•	Full-zip fleece jackets
	•	Textured fleece overshirts
	•	Neutral knit sweaters
	•	Straight-leg trousers and chinos
	•	Dark or medium-wash denim
	•	Simple scarves, beanies, and gloves
	•	Leather boots, understated sneakers, and casual loafers when seasonally appropriate
Statement Pieces
Some looks may feature:
	•	A burgundy fleece jacket
	•	Deep red knitwear under a brown jacket
	•	An olive jacket with a muted red scarf
	•	A textured patterned fleece with an understated earth-tone design
	•	A richer rust or burgundy layer among otherwise neutral outfits
Statement pieces should feel like a natural extension of the brand, not a different fashion aesthetic.
Styling Rules
	•	Clothing should fit naturally, neither extremely oversized nor excessively tight.
	•	Layers should appear practical for cool autumn and early winter weather.
	•	Mix different textures, especially fleece, wool, denim, cotton, and leather.
	•	Favor comfortable, polished outfits over formal tailoring.
	•	Avoid excessive branding and large visible logos.
	•	Avoid heavy streetwear, flashy patterns, neon athleticwear, and overly formal suits.
	•	Fleece must remain the main focus.
05. Photography Direction
Photography Style
Use realistic, editorial lifestyle photography inspired by premium clothing campaigns.
Photographs should appear candid, intimate, and naturally composed.
The images should feel like moments captured during an actual day in New York.
Camera Perspectives
The prompt builder must support:
	1	Lifestyle portraits: One or more subjects photographed naturally in an environment.
	2	Wide environmental shots: Subjects shown within the waterfront landscape.
	3	Medium shots: Upper-body framing that highlights fleece jackets and layers.
	4	Product-focused images: Clear compositions emphasizing a specific garment.
	5	Detail shots: Close-ups of fleece texture, collars, zippers, seams, and cuffs.
	6	Walking shots: Subjects moving naturally along the waterfront.
	7	Seated shots: Relaxed moments on benches, steps, or outdoor café seating.
	8	Over-the-shoulder shots: Looking toward the skyline or water.
	9	Low-angle perspectives: Subtly emphasizing a garment or silhouette.
	10	Group portraits: Friends interacting without stiff or exaggerated posing.
Image Quality
	•	Photorealistic, high-resolution imagery
	•	Believable natural skin texture
	•	Realistic fleece texture and garment construction
	•	Soft depth of field when appropriate
	•	Natural proportions and realistic hands
	•	Editorial framing with intentional negative space
	•	No artificial-looking faces or exaggerated expressions
06. Lighting & Atmosphere
Preferred Lighting
	•	Soft overcast daylight
	•	Cool late-afternoon winter sunlight
	•	Golden-hour lighting near sunset
	•	Muted cloudy skies
	•	Gentle reflections from the water
	•	Subtle warm light from nearby cafés or buildings
Seasonal Atmosphere
The season should feel like late November through early winter.
Environmental details may include:
	•	Nearly bare trees
	•	Fallen brown and golden leaves
	•	Cool, slightly gray skies
	•	Soft waterfront haze
	•	Wind moving through clothing and hair
	•	Calm or gently rippling water
	•	Occasional understated holiday decorations
The campaign should not depend on heavy snowfall or obvious Christmas imagery.
Holiday elements, if used, should be subtle and secondary to the clothing and environment.
07. Locations & Setting
All campaign photography must take place in New York City, near or directly beside the water.
Preferred Locations
Domino Park, Williamsburg, Brooklyn
	•	East River waterfront
	•	Manhattan skyline in the background
	•	Industrial architectural elements
	•	Waterfront railings, walking paths, and benches
Hudson River Park, Manhattan
	•	Riverside walking paths
	•	Open views of the Hudson River
	•	Piers, benches, and waterfront greenery
	•	Late autumn city atmosphere
Brooklyn Bridge Park, Brooklyn
	•	Waterfront promenades
	•	Manhattan skyline and bridge views
	•	Natural urban textures
	•	Relaxed public spaces
Additional Waterfront Settings
	•	Outdoor cafés overlooking the water
	•	Waterfront benches and public seating
	•	Ferry terminals and piers
	•	Waterfront walkways and viewing platforms
Location Rules
	•	New York City must remain geographically recognizable when the shot is wide enough.
	•	Keep the waterfront visible or clearly implied through railings, piers, nearby architecture, or river views.
	•	Do not use rural forests, cabins, mountains, ski resorts, or suburban neighborhoods.
	•	Avoid beach vacation imagery or tropical settings.
	•	Architectural details should feel authentic to NYC.
	•	The setting must support the clothing rather than compete with it.
08. People & Lifestyle
Subjects
Feature a natural mix of adults, typically in their 20s to 40s, representing the diversity of New York City.
Subjects may appear individually, as couples, or in small groups of friends.
Natural Activities
	•	Walking beside the river
	•	Sharing a warm drink
	•	Talking while leaning against a railing
	•	Sitting on a bench
	•	Watching the sunset
	•	Adjusting a fleece collar or scarf
	•	Laughing during a conversation
	•	Taking a casual walk after coffee
Posing Rules
Subjects should look comfortable and genuinely engaged with their surroundings.
Avoid exaggerated fashion poses, artificial smiles, synchronized movements, and overly dramatic expressions.
The clothing should move and fit naturally.
09. Campaign Composition
Each image should have one clear visual focus.
The campaign should alternate between wide lifestyle imagery, portraits, product details, and environmental scenes while maintaining a consistent visual identity.
Composition Rules
	•	Keep fleece garments visible and recognizable.
	•	Maintain a cohesive seasonal color palette.
	•	Avoid crowded backgrounds.
	•	Use natural urban lines, waterfront railings, and the horizon to guide the composition.
	•	Allow intentional negative space for campaign text and logos.
	•	Do not include generated lettering or logos in the source photographs.
	•	Keep the overall photographic style consistent across the campaign.
10. Typography & Graphic Identity
The finished advertisements should follow the established Faherty campaign style guide, existing logo, and holiday logo variation.
Typography should feel understated, polished, and approachable.
Use a combination of the selected campaign fonts, clear hierarchy, and generous spacing.
The campaign title, "Stay a Little Longer," should feel inviting rather than aggressive or overly promotional.
AI-generated photographs should contain no typography. The logo, tagline, and other graphic elements will be added during the final advertising design stage.
11. Advertising Formats
The prompt builder must support different image proportions based on the intended placement.
Supported Formats
	•	NYC subway poster — vertical
	•	Outdoor billboard — panoramic horizontal
	•	Magazine print advertisement — portrait
	•	Instagram feed advertisement — 4:5 portrait
	•	Instagram Story / Reel — 9:16 vertical
	•	Square social media advertisement — 1:1
	•	Website hero image — wide horizontal
The generated prompt must identify the selected format and request appropriate framing and negative space.
Exact physical dimensions should be determined based on the final advertising placement or printer specification.
12. On-Brand vs. Off-Brand
On-Brand
	•	Smart-casual winter outfits
	•	Olive, brown, cream, oatmeal, and charcoal neutrals
	•	Restrained burgundy and red statement pieces
	•	Realistic, detailed fleece textures
	•	New York City waterfront scenery
	•	Candid interactions between friends
	•	Soft autumn and early winter lighting
	•	Understated, premium editorial photography
	•	Cozy and welcoming seasonal atmosphere
Off-Brand
	•	Heavy Christmas decorations in every image
	•	Neon colors or excessive saturation
	•	Ski slopes, mountain cabins, or rural landscapes
	•	Overly formal business attire
	•	Flashy designer styling
	•	Artificial or exaggerated fashion poses
	•	Summer clothing and warm-weather scenery
	•	Heavy snowstorms obscuring the garments
	•	Artificial skin, distorted garments, or unrealistic hands
	•	Generic studio imagery with no NYC waterfront connection
13. Prompt Builder Instructions
The prompt builder should generate detailed, ready-to-use prompts for AI image generation.
Every generated prompt must:
	1	Identify the Faherty "Stay a Little Longer" campaign.
	2	Specify a New York City waterfront location.
	3	Establish the late fall / early winter season.
	4	Describe a smart-casual winter outfit with fleece as a central garment.
	5	Apply the established neutral color palette.
	6	Introduce burgundy or red accents only when appropriate.
	7	Describe the selected photography category and camera angle.
	8	Specify believable lighting and seasonal atmosphere.
	9	Include the requested image aspect ratio or advertising format.
	10	Maintain realistic editorial photography.
	11	Request negative space when needed for ad copy.
	12	Exclude generated logos, lettering, watermarks, and mockup environments.
The tool should allow variation in composition, garments, model interactions, and photography styles without departing from the campaign's visual identity.
Creative principle: Every image should feel like it belongs to the same Faherty fall/winter campaign, even when the people, products, angles, and advertising formats change.
