About this generator & exporting

Inspired by my WITNESS poster study: bold condensed typography, strong contrast, structural bars, and controlled rotation. Generate makes a new poster composition while preserving your words and chosen settings. Live data influences the design; the controls let you take over.

Weather comes from Open-Meteo. Subway alerts come from the MTA’s subway alerts feed. Weather checks every five minutes; MTA checks every minute while the page is visible and automatic refresh is on. Demo values are labeled on the poster.

For your original fonts, keep this page next to the fonts/ folder containing Anton-Regular.ttf, Oswald-VariableFont_wght.ttf, BebasNeue-Regular.ttf, and LeagueGothic-Regular-VariableFont_wdth.ttf, with their licenses. The app also tries Google Fonts if a local file is missing. If fonts cannot load, it identifies the system fallback.

Export saves the exact SVG currently in the preview. Text and shapes remain separate and editable. Install the poster’s font on your computer to check its text in Illustrator; browser font embedding does not replace desktop installation. Changes that cannot fit legibly keep the previous valid poster.
