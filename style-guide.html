My visual direction · 01

A style guide for my AI

CleanSportyStructured

These are the design rules for my class homepage. I want it to feel simple, organized, and easy to use, with a little movement and a pop of green. Follow these decisions every time you build or edit my page.

01

The Page

Leanna Urena
FULL-WIDTH HERO IMAGE

Structure & inspiration

Build a one-page index of my assignments. Put my name at the top left, with a wide hero image underneath and the assignment links below it.

Use Apple as a reference for simplicity, Nike for strong imagery, and Adidas for a sporty, structured feel.

Keep the hero image across the full content width with square corners. Use a 16 / 7 aspect ratio on laptops and tablets and 4 / 3 on phones, with object-fit: cover. Leave 24px between my name and the image.

02

The Palette

Background#FFFFFF
Text#111111
Accent#2E9B4F

White, black & a pop of green

Keep the background white and the text almost black. Use green for links, borders, button fills, and floating background shapes.

Assignment links should have a green fill with black text. Keep other links recognizable with an underline or a green border, and keep small text black for readability.

Use these exact three colours as the core palette. Let spacing and strong alignment keep the page feeling clean.

03

The Typography

Small headings.
Clear direction.
Simple type that lets my work stand out.

Helvetica Neue + Arial

Simple & readable

Use Helvetica Neue for headings, with Helvetica and Arial as fallbacks. Keep headings small and bold at weight 700, with a 1.25 line height.

Set my name to 24px on laptops and 22px on phones. Set section headings to 18px. Use Arial for body text at 16px, weight 400, and a 1.5 line height.

Use 16px bold text for assignment links. Keep labels short and allow longer titles to wrap naturally.

The Details

Interaction & movement

Make assignment links filled buttons with square corners. Each link fills its column, is at least 56px tall, and has 16px vertical and 20px horizontal padding.

On hover, underline the text with a 2px line and a 4px offset. Add the same underline on keyboard focus, plus a 2px black outline with a 3px offset.

Use green circles and squares floating up at a fast speed: 4–6 seconds per pass. Keep them faint, behind the content, and out of the way of clicks. Include a pause button and respect reduced-motion settings.

The build rules

  1. Center content at a maximum width of 1200px. Use 32px side padding on laptops, 24px on tablets, and 16px on phones.
  2. Use three assignment columns at 1024px and above, two from 640–1023px, and one below 640px.
  3. Use 24px grid gaps on laptops and tablets and 16px on phones. Separate major sections by 48px on laptops and 32px on phones.
  4. Use 12 floating shapes on laptops, eight on tablets, and six on phones. Size them at 20–64px with 0.06–0.12 opacity and staggered starts.
  5. Use one main heading, descriptive image alt text, real assignment links, and clearly visible keyboard focus.
  6. Keep these decisions consistent whenever new assignments are added. This guide uses two columns to compare examples; the homepage assignment grid follows rule 2.
